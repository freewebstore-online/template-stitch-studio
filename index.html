FAQ

Before you come in

Do I need an appointment? +

No — walk in any time during opening hours for a quote and a fitting. Bridal and made-to-measure work is booked in advance so we can give it a full appointment.

How long will it take? +

Most alterations are ready within three to seven days. Simple hems can often be done same-day if the bench is clear — ask when you drop off.

What should I bring with me? +

The shoes and any underlayers you will wear with the garment. Hem length and jacket balance both change with footwear, so fitting without them means guessing.

What if the fit still is not right? +

Bring it straight back. Adjustments to work we have done are re-fitted at no charge — we would rather fix it than have you wear something that does not sit properly.

Can you save a garment that is badly damaged? +

Often, yes — moth holes, tears and worn linings are all repairable. Bring it in and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth the cost before starting.
